Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 14, 2025

BYD Launcher respects your privacy. The app requests only the minimum permissions necessary to provide its features, and never transmits your personal data to third parties or stores it on external servers.

1. Data We Access and Why

Data Permission Purpose Transmitted?
Location (city level) ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION
ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION		 Obtain latitude/longitude for weather lookup Coordinates only sent to the Open-Meteo API (city-level precision)
Calendar events READ_CALENDAR Display today's events (title and time) Never — processed on-device only
Installed app list — Populate the app drawer and favorites grid Never
Favorites selection — Store chosen app package names on-device Never
Drive session data — Record eco-drive sessions (score, event counts) in local Room database Never — stored locally only

2. Third-Party Services

BYD Launcher uses Open-Meteo for weather data. When weather is requested, your device's latitude and longitude are sent to Open-Meteo servers. Please review their privacy policy below.

  • Open-Meteo Terms & Privacy

The app contains no analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, crash-reporting services, or any other third-party tracking libraries.

3. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Location data is used only at the moment of a weather request and is never persisted inside the app.
  • Your favorites list (package names) is stored in an on-device DataStore and is deleted when the app is uninstalled.
  • Calendar data is read for display purposes only and is not stored separately.
  • Drive session records are stored in a local Room database on the device and are deleted when the app is uninstalled.

4. Behavior When Permissions Are Denied

  • Location permission denied — The weather card displays a "Location permission required" message. All other features continue to work normally.
  • Calendar permission denied — The calendar card shows no events. All other features continue to work normally.

5. Children's Privacy

BYD Launcher is designed for use inside vehicle infotainment systems and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page in the GitHub repository. Continued use of the app after a policy change constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

7. Contact

For questions or concerns about this privacy policy, please open an issue on GitHub.
